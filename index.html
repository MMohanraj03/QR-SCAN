Mobile Phone Scanning Setup

When scanning the QR code using a real smartphone camera, the phone needs to reach your computer's server on the same Wi-Fi network.

💡 Scanning from the same computer?
You can keep the default localhost or click "Test Scan / Open Resume".
📱 Scanning with your physical mobile phone?
Replace localhost with your computer's local Wi-Fi IP address (e.g. 192.168.1.100:5173) so your phone's browser can connect.
